How CGT is calculated on a £{{ "{:,}".format(gain) }} gain

The annual exempt amount for 2026/27 is £3,000. After deducting this, the taxable gain is £{{ "{:,}".format(calc_basic.taxable_gain) }}. Gains within your remaining basic-rate band are charged at 18%. Gains above the basic-rate band are charged at 24%.

Your other taxable income (salary, self-employment, etc.) fills the basic-rate band first. A basic-rate taxpayer on £35,000 has £{{ "{:,}".format(50270 - 35000) }} of basic-rate band remaining — so smaller gains may fall entirely within the 18% band. A higher-rate taxpayer on £55,000 has already exceeded the £50,270 limit, so gains are charged at 24%.
